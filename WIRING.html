Touch Remote Wiring

12 wires. The screen and its touch chip share the clock (SCK) and data (SDI) lines, so two of the ESP32-S3 pins get two wires each. A breadboard row makes that easy. Tick each wire off as you go. Your ticks are saved in this browser.

ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1 N16R8 Hosyond 4.0" 480×320 SPI touch display Breadboard + 12 jumper wires

Leave SDO (MISO) empty. On many of these 4" boards the screen's SDO pin keeps talking when it shouldn't and garbles the touch readings. The screen doesn't need it; only the touch chip's T_DO goes to pin 13. Plug the S3's USB into the port labeled COM (or UART) for the first flash.

First power-up: what you should see

  1. The openHASP logo, then a QR code and a Wi-Fi name starting with HASP-. Join it from your phone (password haspadmin) and pick your home Wi-Fi.
  2. The screen shows its new address. Open it in a browser to reach the remote's settings.
  3. Set the rotation to landscape, then calibrate touch: tap the crosshairs as they appear.
  4. Blank white screen: the backlight works but the picture doesn't. Check CS, DC and RESET. Nothing at all: check VCC, GND and LED.
  5. Touch lands in the wrong spot or not at all: run calibrate again, then check T_CS and T_DO.
